## 🎬 Recording Tips

1. **Screen resolution**: Record at **1920×1080** (Full HD) for best quality
2. **Browser**: Use Chrome with a clean tab — hide bookmarks bar
3. **Clear localStorage** before recording: Open DevTools (F12) → Application → Storage → Clear site data — this ensures you start on the login page
4. **Mouse movements**: Move the cursor slowly and deliberately — avoid jerky movements
5. **Zoom**: Use browser zoom at **100%** or **110%** for better visibility
6. **Voiceover**: Record narration separately for cleaner audio — then sync
7. **Tools**: OBS Studio (free) for screen recording, or use the built-in Xbox Game Bar (Win + G)
8. **Background music**: Add subtle, royalty-free background music at low volume during editing
